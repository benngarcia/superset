## All Your Automation Runs <PRBadge url="https://github.com/superset-sh/superset/pull/7773" /> <PRBadge url="https://github.com/superset-sh/superset/pull/8074" />

Find failed runs and retry your automations from one place in **Automations → All runs**.

- Filter to your runs or the whole team's, then inspect what triggered a run
- Select failed runs to retry their automations together
- Click the sidebar's failure badge to go straight to your failed runs

![All runs filtered to automations that need a retry](/changelog/2026-10-04-automation-history.png)

Docs: [Automations](https://docs.superset.sh/docs/automations).

## Linear Issues, Live <PRBadge url="https://github.com/superset-sh/superset/pull/8070" /> <PRBadge url="https://github.com/superset-sh/superset/pull/8094" /> <PRBadge url="https://github.com/superset-sh/superset/pull/7963" />

Browse and create Linear issues directly in **Tasks → Linear**, with list and board views, team and assignee filters, and an option to import an issue as a separate Superset task.

- Each member connects their own Linear account
- Superset tasks now have short team IDs such as `ENG-123`

## Restore Deleted Projects <PRBadge url="https://github.com/superset-sh/superset/pull/7896" />

Deleted a project by mistake? Click **Undo** or restore it for up to 30 days from **Settings → Projects → Recently deleted**.

Docs: [Workspaces](https://docs.superset.sh/docs/workspaces).

## Improvements

- **Review code beside your agent** - open **Code** in the workspace's PR pane to see changed files, diffs, and review threads <PRBadge url="https://github.com/superset-sh/superset/pull/8007" />
- **Pages remember team input** - ask your agent to build a poll, RSVP sheet, or shared checklist as a [Page](https://docs.superset.sh/docs/pages); signed-in teammates' answers persist and update live <PRBadge url="https://github.com/superset-sh/superset/pull/8015" />
- **New models** - choose GPT-6.1 Sol or Claude Sonnet 5.5 in the agent model picker; GPT-6.1 Sol support contributed by [@joshrchambers](https://github.com/joshrchambers) <PRBadge url="https://github.com/superset-sh/superset/pull/8009" /> <PRBadge url="https://github.com/superset-sh/superset/pull/7935" />
- **File and browser preferences** - find auto save in **Settings → Files & Editor** and URL handling in **Settings → Browser** <PRBadge url="https://github.com/superset-sh/superset/pull/7916" />
- **Invite from people pickers** - add a teammate without leaving the person picker you're using <PRBadge url="https://github.com/superset-sh/superset/pull/7943" />
- **Shared terminal sizing** - a banner explains when a phone or smaller window is making your terminal narrower <PRBadge url="https://github.com/superset-sh/superset/pull/7996" />
- **Integration plan changes** - Linear and GitHub integrations require Pro; free organizations see the upgrade option in **Settings → Integrations** <PRBadge url="https://github.com/superset-sh/superset/pull/7985" /> <PRBadge url="https://github.com/superset-sh/superset/pull/8048" />
- **CLI help for your shell** - generate completions, inspect available commands, or check your configuration with the commands below; [CLI docs](https://docs.superset.sh/docs/cli/cli-reference) <PRBadge url="https://github.com/superset-sh/superset/pull/8030" />

```sh
superset completion zsh
superset schema
superset config check
```

- **Experimental: cloud automations** - users with cloud access can choose **Cloud** in the automation editor to start a fresh workspace or continue in an existing one <PRBadge url="https://github.com/superset-sh/superset/pull/7922" />
- **Experimental: agent chat** - users with chat enabled can start Claude, Codex, or Pi in chat from the new-workspace screen, with attachments and agent-provided model choices <PRBadge url="https://github.com/superset-sh/superset/pull/8058" />
